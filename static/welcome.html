Welcome to Valet

An AI agent that works in your own Chrome, with the model you bring. Three things and you're set.

1

Open the side panel

Valet lives in Chrome's side panel, next to whatever page you're on. Click the Valet icon in the toolbar to open it — pin it from the puzzle-piece menu 🧩 so it stays there.

2

Connect a model

Sign in with a Claude or ChatGPT subscription, use an API key, or point it at Ollama on this computer. Keys and sign-ins stay encrypted in the extension; page content goes only to the model you pick.

Once connected, pick a model on its row.

3

Try a first task

Open any page, then say what you want done in the panel. It works in that tab while you watch — a glowing border and its own cursor — and asks before it opens a new site or submits a form. Some first tasks:

  • Find the cheapest blue ceramic mug on this page and tell me its price.
  • Take a screenshot of this page and describe what's above the fold.
  • Fill this form with my name, Amen, and stop before submitting.

Under the message box: Ask/Auto sets how often it checks with you, Learn makes it explain and wait before every step, and @ brings in a skill — a note you write once for a site (Settings → Skills).

You can open this guide again from Settings in the side panel.
